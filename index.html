Welcome to Vice State - Next-Gen Open World Gaming Experience

🚀 Gameplay Features

Dual Protagonists

  • Lucia & Jason: Play as the first dual-protagonist duo inspired by Bonnie and Clyde.
  • Dynamic character switching during missions and free-roam.
  • Shared inventory and tactical combo abilities.

Advanced AI & Physics

  • Next-generation NPC intelligence with realistic routine behaviors.
  • Enhanced vehicle damage physics, water simulation, and weather dynamics.
  • Realistic police response tactics and surveillance mechanics.

Social Media Integration

  • In-game viral TikTok/Instagram style live streaming platform.
  • NPCs react to players and record events on smartphones.
  • Dynamic news and social media influence on mission outcomes.

🌴 World & Detailing

State of Leonida (Vice City)

  • Expanded Vice City with surrounding wetlands (Grassrivers).
  • Includes surrounding towns, beaches, and dense urban sprawl.
  • Largest and most detailed map in Rockstar Games history.

Interiors & Heists

  • Over 60% of buildings feature fully accessible interiors.
  • Robbery systems: Pawn shops, convenience stores, and armored trucks.
  • Pawn system to buy, sell, and trade stolen goods.

Vehicle Customization

  • Deep interior and exterior vehicle tuning options.
  • Lowriders, mudding trucks, speedboats, and jet skis.
  • Realistic interior dash cams and customizable mirrors.
